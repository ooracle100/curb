How this is measured

The premium is the gap between what a dollar of USDT trades for on peer-to-peer markets and the reference rate published for that currency. A premium of 8% means buyers are paying 8% more than the reference rate to get hold of dollars.

The peer-to-peer figure is the median of the ten deepest sell adverts on the venue named beside each market, taken once a day. The reference rate comes from open.er-api.com, which aggregates published rates. It is not a central bank rate, and on days when a central bank rate diverges from it, the premium shown here will understate the official gap.
